When is Reranking NOT enough?

Reranking operates exclusively on the top-K candidate pool fetched by the initial retriever. It fails if: (1) Retriever Failure: The gold chunk was completely missed in the initial top-K (Recall = 0); (2) Chunk Fragmentation: Crucial clauses are broken across chunks; (3) Semantic Drift: User query uses synonyms or colloquialisms not captured by keyword indexing. In those cases, Hybrid Search (Dense + Sparse) or Query Expansion (HyDE) is required.

LLM-as-a-Judge Rubric & Bias Detection

Evaluating language models with other language models requires calibrated rubrics and active bias mitigation. The evaluator itself is not automatically neutral.

OrbitTech Store Customer Support Rubric (1–5 Thang điểm chuẩn hóa)
Judge Bias Monitor (Wiring to LLMJudge.detect_bias)
Positional Bias

The judge systematically favors whichever candidate response appears in Position 1. Detected when Position 1 mean score exceeds Position 2 by > 0.15.

Remedy: Pairwise order swap with consensus averaging.
Leniency Bias

The judge is overly generous, awarding passing marks to answers with subtle inaccuracies. Detected when average batch score > 0.8 across criteria.

Remedy: Few-shot calibration against human ground-truth labels.
Severity Bias

The judge is overly critical, failing valid concise responses. Detected when average batch score < 0.3 across criteria.

Remedy: Explicit rubric instructions to reward concise factual completeness.
